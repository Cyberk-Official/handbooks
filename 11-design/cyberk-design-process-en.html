Principles

How we work

Three principles

01

Structure first, colour later.

We lock flows and structure in black and white before investing in colour and imagery.

02

Validate early.

We test with real users and review feasibility while changes are still cheap.

03

You approve every gate.

Every step ends with a clear approval gate, so there are no surprises at the end.
