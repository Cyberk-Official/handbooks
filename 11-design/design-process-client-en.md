## 4 steps from idea to product

Each step ends with a gate where you review, comment and approve before we move on.

01. **Discovery & Prototype**: Research your market and users, then turn it into a clickable blueprint of your product
02. **Visual Direction & Key Mockups**: See your product in your brand's look on a few key screens, then pick a direction
03. **Design System**: One rulebook for how your product looks, so it stays consistent and AI tools can build it faster
04. **Detailed UI & Testing**: Every screen finished, including the moments most apps forget: no internet, nothing to show, something went wrong

---

## Three principles

1. **Structure first, colour later.** We lock flows and structure in black and white before investing in colour and imagery.
2. **Validate early.** We test with real users and review feasibility while changes are still cheap.
3. **You approve every gate.** Every step ends with a clear approval gate, so there are no surprises at the end.

## Step 01 · Discovery & Prototype
*Right problem, right flow*

_Illustration: research card → wireframe phone → wireframe list screen_

**What we do**
- Review your business goals, requirements and brand
- Research your market, competitors and the apps your users already know
- Talk to target users, or analyse the feedback you already have
- Map user flows and wireframe every screen
- Link them into a clickable prototype and test it with users

**What you get**
- Market & competitor research
- User personas
- User flows & wireframes
- Clickable prototype
- Test findings

**What we need from you:** Business documents, brand guidelines, access to real users if available, and feedback on the flows.

**Approval gate:** Goals, flows & screen list agreed

---

## Step 02 · Visual Direction & Key Mockups
*Test the look on a few screens*

_Illustration: moodboard + two coloured key screens_

**What we do**
- Build a moodboard: colour, type and imagery style
- Fully design 2–4 core screens
- Check contrast and visual hierarchy, and preview on a real phone

**What you get**
- Visual direction moodboard
- 2–4 finished key screens

**What we need from you:** Your impressions and your choice of the direction that best fits your brand.

**Approval gate:** Visual direction approved

---

## Step 03 · Design System
*One rulebook, faster build*

_Illustration: design system board: colours, type scale, components_

**What we do**
- Set the rules for colours, fonts and spacing
- Create a ready-made set of buttons, forms and cards to reuse on every screen
- Organise everything so AI coding tools can read it and turn it into code
- Make sure text is easy to read and buttons are easy to tap

**What you get**
- A complete style library in Figma
- Files ready for your developers and AI tools
- A simple guide on how to use it

**What we need from you:** Nothing required. You are welcome to review and comment.

**Approval gate:** Design standards confirmed

---

## Step 04 · Detailed UI & Testing
*No screen left unfinished*

_Illustration: three edge-case screens: no internet, empty, error_

**What we do**
- Finish every remaining screen in your product
- Design the moments most apps forget: no internet, nothing to show yet, errors
- Polish every detail so the whole app feels finished
- Do a final check with real content on real phones

**What you get**
- Every screen of your app, finished
- A polished, consistent look throughout
- Files ready to hand to your developers

**What we need from you:** Review each screen and send us the final wording.

**Approval gate:** Design signed off for development
