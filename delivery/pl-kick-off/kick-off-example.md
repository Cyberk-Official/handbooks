# Kick-off với Khách hàng — Mẫu Agenda, Tin nhắn & Email

**Cập nhật lần cuối:** 2026-08-27
**Trạng thái:** Nháp

---

## 1. Tin nhắn sáng ngày Internal Planning (Bước 1)

> Gửi cho client qua Telegram hoặc Email — **trước** khi team bắt đầu họp nội bộ.

```
Hi [Client Name],

Good morning! I'm [PL Name], your Product Lead from Cyberk — 
I'll be your main point of contact throughout the project. 
Very excited to be working with you on [Project Name]!

I wanted to share how we kick things off at Cyberk, so you know 
exactly what to expect:

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📋 Step 1 — Internal Planning (today)

Today, our full team — developers, QA, and tech lead — will sit 
down together to go through the project requirements in detail. 
We'll build a complete project plan: task breakdown, timeline, 
milestones, risk assessment, and team assignments.

Why we do this: We want every team member to deeply understand 
the product before writing a single line of code. This session 
ensures everyone is aligned — not just on WHAT to build, but 
WHY it matters to your business.

I'll send you the Planning Report by end of day today.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🤝 Step 2 — Kick-off Call (with you)

After the plan is ready, I'd like to schedule a kick-off call 
where we:
  • Introduce the team — so you know who's working on what
  • Walk through the project plan in detail
  • Discuss our approach and get your feedback

This call is important because it's where we align on 
expectations, communication rhythm, and make sure we're 
100% on the same page before Sprint 1 begins.

Could you share your availability for a 45–60 min call 
in the next 2–3 days? I'll send an agenda beforehand 
so you know exactly what we'll cover.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Looking forward to a great collaboration!

Best,
[PL Name]
```

## 2. Tin nhắn gửi kèm Planning Report (Bước 2)

> Gửi **ngay sau** Internal Planning hoàn tất. Không gửi file trơn — phải kèm summary.

```
Hi [Client Name],

Our internal planning session is complete. 
Please find the attached Planning Report for [Project Name].

Here's a quick summary:

📋 Approach
- [1–2 câu mô tả cách team sẽ tiếp cận dự án]
- Example: "We'll follow an agile approach with 2-week sprints, 
  starting with the core user flow before building out secondary features."

⚠️ Key Risks
- [Risk 1]: [Mitigation plan]
- [Risk 2]: [Mitigation plan]
- Example: "Third-party API documentation is incomplete — 
  we've allocated a spike in Sprint 1 to validate integration."

🎯 Strategy
- [1–2 câu về chiến lược delivery]
- Example: "We prioritize the Key Success Features for the first 
  3 sprints to ensure the core value is deliverable on time."

I'd like to schedule a kick-off call with you to walk through 
the plan in detail, introduce the team, and discuss the approach. 
Would any of the following times work?

  • [Option 1: Date, Time]
  • [Option 2: Date, Time]
  • [Option 3: Date, Time]

Best,
[PL Name]
```

## 3. Agenda gửi cho Client trước buổi Kick-off Call (Bước 3)

> Gửi khi client confirm lịch — ít nhất **1 ngày trước** buổi call.

```
Subject: [Project Name] — Kick-off Meeting Agenda

Hi [Client Name],

Below is the agenda for our kick-off meeting on [Date], [Time]:

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  KICK-OFF MEETING — [Project Name]
  [Date] · [Time] · [Duration: 45–60 min]
  [Google Meet / Zoom link]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. Team Introduction                          10 min
     Meet the Cyberk team — who does what,
     who to contact for what.

  2. Project Planning Walk-through              15 min
     Detailed review of the Planning Report:
     — Scope & deliverables
     — Timeline & milestones
     — Feature prioritization
     — Task breakdown & ownership

  3. Approach & Strategy Discussion             15 min
     How we plan to tackle the project:
     — Development methodology (Agile/Sprint)
     — Technical approach & architecture
     — Risk mitigation strategy
     — Communication & reporting cadence

  4. Open Q&A                                   10 min
     Your questions, concerns, and anything
     you'd like us to adjust.

  5. Next Steps & Action Items                   5 min
     Confirm start date, communication channels,
     and immediate action items.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Attendees from Cyberk:
  • [PL Name] — Product Lead
  • [TL Name] — Tech Lead
  • [Dev Name] — Developer (if applicable)

Please let us know if there are topics you'd like to add.

Best,
[PL Name]
```

## 4. Email tổng kết sau buổi Kick-off Call (Bước 5)

> Gửi **trong ngày** sau buổi call.

```
Subject: [Project Name] — Kick-off Meeting Summary & Next Steps

Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for the kick-off meeting today. Below is a summary 
of what we discussed and agreed upon.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ATTENDEES
  Cyberk: [Names]
  Client: [Names]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

KEY POINTS DISCUSSED

1. Team Introduction
   - [PL] leads project communication
   - [TL] leads technical delivery
   - [Dev/QA names if introduced]

2. Planning
   - Timeline: [Start date] → [End date]
   - Sprint cadence: [2-week sprints]
   - Key milestones: [list]

3. Approach
   - [Summary of agreed approach]
   - [Any adjustments from the discussion]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ACTION ITEMS

| # | Item | Owner | Due |
|---|------|-------|-----|
| 1 | [e.g. Client to provide API credentials] | [Client] | [Date] |
| 2 | [e.g. Cyberk to set up dev environment] | [TL] | [Date] |
| 3 | [e.g. Schedule weekly sync call] | [PL] | [Date] |

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NEXT STEPS
  • Sprint 1 starts: [Date]
  • First progress report: [Date]
  • Communication channel: [Telegram group]
  • Weekly sync: [Day, Time]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Attached: Planning Report (PDF), Meeting slides

Please let us know if anything needs to be corrected.

Best,
[PL Name]
```
